Pianissimo offline Checking network

Swedish speech to text, on this computer

Both apps run Klang's open Pianissimo model locally. Turn the Wi-Fi off first, they work the same.

App 1

Podcast summary

Drop in an episode. Get the full transcript, a summary and chapters you can click.

App 2

Offline dictation

Talk, and the text appears while you speak. Flight mode on.

App 3

Show archive

Paste a show. Every episode is transcribed overnight, then search all of them at once.

App 4

Compare two episodes

Two episodes on one subject: what they agree on, where they differ, and what only one says.
